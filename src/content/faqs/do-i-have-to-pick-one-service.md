question: Do I have to pick just one service?
topic: process
order: 110
showOn: [services]
---

No. You can hire MIS for one defined service, such as a website, SEO assessment, email campaign, integration, or AI consultation.

Some problems cross service boundaries. A search issue may require changes to the website, or a new store may need email and point-of-sale integration. When that happens, we explain which pieces belong together and which can wait.

You are not required to buy a package or commit to future services.
