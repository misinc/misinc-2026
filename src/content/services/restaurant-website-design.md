## Every café, restaurant, and food truck needs a real website

Not a social media page. A website — because that is where people go to check whether you are open, whether you deliver, and whether the thing they remember eating is still on the menu.

The catch is that restaurant websites go stale faster than any other kind. Prices change, specials change, hours change around holidays. A site only you can update is a site that will be wrong within a month.

## Built for the way restaurants actually work

We build restaurant sites on platforms your staff can manage from a phone during a slow hour:

- **Menus you edit yourself.** Change a price, mark something sold out, add a special — in under a minute, with no developer involved.
- **Online ordering, pickup, and delivery**, connected to the point-of-sale system you already run.
- **Catering enquiries** that arrive as a proper request with the details you need, not a vague voicemail.
- **Gift cards**, which sell far better online than most owners expect.
- **Hours and holiday changes** you control, so Google shows the truth.

## Working with Square

We are a Square Online certified agency, and for restaurants already running Square as their POS it is usually the right answer. Your menu, inventory, and online orders live in one system, which means the online store and the register cannot disagree with each other.

If you use something else, that is fine — we will work with what you have rather than making a platform migration a condition of a new website.
