## The problem with WordPress

WordPress powers a huge share of the web, and that popularity is exactly why it is targeted. Plugins go out of date, vulnerabilities get published, and automated attacks find unpatched sites within hours.

Most business owners do not have the time or the inclination to track any of that. That is what ProCare is for. No matter where your WordPress site is hosted, we take these off your plate:

- **24/7 website edits.** Email us your changes and we make them, whether it is 3pm or 3am.
- **Security hardening.** Continuous protection and monitoring against the attacks WordPress sites actually face.
- **Speed optimization.** Caching and image optimization configured properly, not just installed.
- **Uptime monitoring.** We check your site around the clock and act within a minute of it going down — usually before you know anything happened.
- **Weekly updates.** Plugins, themes, and core files updated and verified, with a report so you can see what changed.
- **Cloud backups.** Full off-site backups with one-click restore.
- **Advanced support.** WooCommerce stores, membership sites, and custom builds included.

## Why this is worth paying for

A hacked site is not just an inconvenience. It means downtime, a Google warning label in front of your listing, and a cleanup that costs far more than a year of maintenance. Most of the emergency recoveries we get called for would have been prevented by an update that was available weeks earlier.
