## Patients arrive with practical questions

Before contacting a practice, people may want to know whether you provide a particular service, accept their insurance, see new patients, or offer a way to request an appointment. They may be comparing several practices from a phone while already concerned about a health issue.

The website should make those answers easy to find without oversimplifying the care you provide.

## What we build for practices

- **Clear service information.** Conditions, procedures, providers, locations, and patient resources are organized around the questions people bring to the site.
- **Visible next steps.** Appointment requests, phone numbers, directions, and patient portal links appear where patients are likely to need them.
- **Accessible foundations.** We address semantic structure, color contrast, keyboard use, focus behavior, alternative text, readable forms, and screen-reader support as part of the design and build. Automated checks can help find issues, but they are not a substitute for thoughtful implementation and manual review.
- **Privacy-conscious forms.** We determine what information a public website form really needs to collect and avoid sending sensitive information through inappropriate tools. Requirements involving protected health information must be evaluated across the complete workflow, including vendors and storage.
- **Strong mobile performance.** Important information should remain usable on a small screen and an imperfect connection.
- **Search-ready service pages.** Clear, specific pages help patients and search systems understand what the practice offers and where it is available.

## Experience with healthcare organizations

Our healthcare work includes [Coleman Vision](/works/colemanvision), [New Mexico Cancer Research Alliance](/works/nm-cancer-research-alliance), and the [Dermatology & Skin Cancer Center of New Mexico](/works/skin-cancer-center-of-new-mexico). These relationships include redesigns, ongoing support, and specialized tools such as NMCRA’s clinical-trials search.

Healthcare website decisions carry responsibilities beyond appearance. We ask where information goes, who maintains it, and what patients need before recommending a design or feature.
