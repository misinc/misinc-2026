## Agents that produce something

Most AI tools give you text back and leave the actual work to you. [Hyperagent](https://hyperagent.com) is built around finished output: a working web page, a document, a dashboard, a video package — produced by agents that can use real browsers, real tooling, and access to the systems you already run.

## Why we use it

**The output is a deliverable, not a draft.** A research task ends with a document you can send, not a wall of notes to reformat.

**It works with your stack.** Agents use your data and your tools rather than operating in isolation, which is what makes the output specific to your business instead of generic.

**Work stays current.** An agent can keep a deliverable up to date as the underlying information changes, rather than producing something that is accurate for a week.

**It reaches people where they already are** — delivered into Slack or email rather than requiring anyone to log into another dashboard.

## What we do with it

We scope the task, build and configure the agent, connect it to the right data, and test it against real cases before it goes near production. Then we watch what it handles and what it escalates, and tune it.

That last part is the difference between an agent that helps and one that quietly makes things worse. An agent nobody reviews is a liability.

## Where it fits

Recurring work that ends in a deliverable: market research, listing packages, candidate shortlists, reporting, campaign material. If your bottleneck is a conversation rather than a document, a different approach usually fits better — see [AI consulting](/services/ai-consulting).

## What to know

This is custom development work. It carries a build cost, needs someone to own it, and works best when it starts narrow: one clearly defined task you can evaluate within a week.
