## The same questions, over and over

Every service business has them. What are your hours, do you cover my area, how much does it cost, how do I book. They arrive by email and through contact forms, often outside office hours, and each one waits until somebody has a moment.

[Add On AI Chat](https://addonaichat.com) answers them immediately, using your own website content as its source.

## How it works

**It learns from your site.** The widget crawls your existing pages and answers from what is actually there, not from a general model guessing about your business.

**It works around the clock.** Someone comparing three companies at 9pm gets an answer from you and silence from the other two.

**It captures leads.** A conversation that reaches the limits of what it can answer turns into a lead with the context attached, rather than a visitor who leaves.

**It works on any platform.** WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Framer, Weebly: it drops into the site you already have. No rebuild.

**We set it up.** This is a done-for-you service, not a subscription you have to configure yourself. We install it, train it on your content, and tune the answers.

## Same principle as our other AI work

An agent that invents an answer is worse than no agent. Add On AI Chat answers from your documented content and hands off when it does not know, which is the same rule we apply to every [AI agent](/services/ai-agents) we build.

## Where it fits

Best for service businesses, multi-location teams, and anyone with steady support volume. If your enquiries are complex and low-volume, a chat widget is not the answer and we will tell you so.
