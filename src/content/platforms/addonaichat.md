## The same questions, over and over

Many organizations answer the same practical questions each week. What are your hours, do you cover my area, how much does it cost, how do I book? They often arrive outside office hours and wait until somebody has time to respond.

[Add On AI Chat](https://addonaichat.com) was our response to the products we found on the market. They were too expensive and included too many confusing features for the small businesses and nonprofits we work with. We built a more focused service around the questions those organizations needed answered.

## How it works

**It learns from your site.** The widget crawls your existing pages and answers from that material, not from a general model guessing about your business.

**It works around the clock.** Visitors can get answers to documented questions even when the office is closed.

**It captures leads.** A conversation that reaches the limits of what it can answer turns into a lead with the context attached, rather than a visitor who leaves.

**It works across common website platforms.** WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Framer, and Weebly sites can add it without a rebuild.

**We set it up.** This is a done-for-you service rather than another subscription to configure alone. We install it, connect it to the approved content, and tune its responses.

## Same principle as our other AI work

Add On AI Chat is designed to answer from documented content and hand off when it does not know. That is the same rule we apply to every [AI agent](/services/ai-agents) we build. The [portfolio note](/works/addonaichat) covers why we chose to build the product ourselves.

## Where it fits

It is best suited to service businesses, nonprofits, multi-location teams, and organizations with recurring questions. If inquiries are complex and infrequent, a chat widget may not be the right investment.
