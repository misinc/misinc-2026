Are you looking to hire an Albuquerque web design company? Here are things you should know before choosing one.

**Robust portfolio**

Any web design company that has been in business for a while should be proud to show off its portfolios. A collection of the past websites the company has created will give you a clear picture of what to expect. Take your time to go through the company portfolio and determine if the company has what you are looking for. Avoid any web design companies that refuse to show you their past work.

**Ongoing Support**

Your chosen Albuquerque web design company should be able to offer continued support in case you can't fix something yourself when your website goes live. Did they explain the functionality of your website? Did they explain what to do if you're ever having trouble? Do you know who to call if you are having issues with your website? The best web design company will set up a chain of communication should you run into an issue.

**Experience**

You want a design firm that's experienced. To justify the company experience, you need to know the number of working hours each team member has or the whole team's combined working hours. You can also look at the number of clients the web design company has worked with. Choosing a team that has been working together for a long period is the best.

**Budget**

It's a good idea to figure out the amount you pay. Don't be fooled by a cheap company; it means you'll receive a low-quality website and you will be charged for additional features. You can understand what a fair estimate should be when you do a little cost research in advance.

**Completion Time**

This is another vital factor when searching for the best web design company in Albuquerque. How long will the website design process take? A company needs its online presence as soon as possible. Therefore, it is essential to have an estimated timeline to complete the work. When the web design company knows the exact time they are supposed to deliver, you can decide if you're willing to wait for the firm.
