Cheezie Chews were edible dog chews made from Himalayan cheese. They were protein-rich and lactose-free, with an ingredient story that distinguished them from common corn-and-starch alternatives.

Pet owners comparing products read ingredient lists, so we led with what the chews contained instead of relying on lifestyle photography to make the case.

This was the third website we built with Brett and the GlobalOne Pet team. That repeat work is the most useful outcome to record. The first project is a test; the third is a working relationship.

The brand has since wound down, and the site is no longer online.
