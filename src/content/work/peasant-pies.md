Square recommended MIS to Peasant Pies. The restaurant already used Square for its point of sale and online store, while the main website remained separate on Wix.

That setup created two places to maintain and two versions of the same information. We migrated the website to [Square Online](/platforms/square-online-store), bringing the menu, inventory, orders, and customer-facing site into the system the restaurant already used.

Ali and the Peasant Pies team were unusually easy and pleasant to work with. As the project developed, a few additional elements made sense, and we were happy to include them without changing the original quote. Their review records that generosity from the client’s side, which is where the story belongs.
