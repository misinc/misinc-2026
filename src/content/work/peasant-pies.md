Square recommended us to Peasant Pies, who were running their point-of-sale and online store through Square while their website sat separately on Wix.

Two systems meant two places to update and two versions of the truth. We migrated the site onto Square Online so the menu, inventory, and orders live in one system.

The quote was fixed at the start. The client added elements during the project and we invoiced the original figure anyway, which is the part they mention in their review, and which is simply how we prefer to work.
