Syserco makes buildings intelligent — energy management systems, controls, and integration across the San Francisco Bay Area, with more than two hundred energy management professionals.

## Organised by building, not by product

The instinct on a project like this is to structure the site around what the company sells: the control systems, the analytics dashboards, the lighting, the field devices.

We structured it around building types instead — bio and pharmaceutical labs, commercial offices, corporate campuses, data centres, government and municipalities, healthcare, higher education, hospitality, K-12 schools, and venues.

The reason is simple. A facilities director at a hospital does not want to read about control systems in the abstract. They want to see that this firm has done hospitals. Sorting the work by building type lets a visitor find their own situation in about ten seconds, and the products remain available for the people who came looking for a specific line.

## Proof, stated plainly

This is a category where everyone claims capability, so the site leans on countable things: LEED accredited professionals on staff, LEED Platinum buildings delivered, the first net zero office building, and tens of millions of square feet of commercial office space under management.

The recruiting story is real too, and unusual enough to deserve space — Syserco has been voted a Best Place to Work in the Bay Area by the *San Francisco Business Times* and the *Silicon Valley Business Journal* year after year since 2011. In a trade with a labour shortage, that is a business asset, not a vanity badge.
