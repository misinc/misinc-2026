We were genuinely pleased to be selected for this one. New Mexico chile is not a small thing here, and the association exists in part to protect it — the NM Certified Chile programme is how buyers know they are getting chile actually grown in New Mexico rather than something labelled to imply it.

The new site puts that programme front and centre and adds a locator so people can find certified growers and products.

A trade association site serves growers, buyers, and the public at once. The structure had to make each of those groups feel the site was built for them.
