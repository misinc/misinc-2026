Lenann McGookey Gardner coaches senior executives, people accustomed to being the most capable person in the room who are now considering whether to ask for help.

That audience is unlikely to respond to familiar coaching slogans. They want evidence of judgment, a clear sense of who Lenann works with, and no hard sell. We designed the site to establish credibility quickly, explain the engagement, and then get out of the way.

The site is no longer online, but Lenann’s review preserves the most important part of the project. Her comments focus on responsiveness, trust, and practical guidance, which remain the standards MIS brings to every client relationship.
