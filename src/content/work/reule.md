Reule had a website that worked when it was built and had stopped working well on the device most of their customers now use.

The redesign was primarily about getting the fundamentals right: fast on a phone, clear about what they do and where they do it, and easy to contact from any page.

For a trade business, that last point carries most of the value. Someone with a broken window is not browsing — they want a phone number, immediately.
