Pars Cuisine is one of Albuquerque’s longstanding Persian and Mediterranean restaurants. Its website needs to welcome first-time guests, answer practical questions before a visit, and make takeout straightforward for returning customers.

We built the main restaurant site on [Weebly Cloud](/platforms/weebly-cloud), with room for the menu, catering information, location details, and the character of the restaurant itself. Pars To Go is handled through [Ecwid](/platforms/ecwid), giving customers a dedicated online ordering path without reducing the whole site to a storefront.

Separating those roles keeps the experience clear. People planning a meal can learn about the restaurant, while someone ready to order can move directly into the Ecwid store. Staff can update day-to-day information without relying on MIS for routine changes.
