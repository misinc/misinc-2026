UR Art & Culture is an Albuquerque nonprofit founded by the composer and oud player Rahim AlHaj to fund and run the Rahim AlHaj Scholarship for Art & Culture, an award for emerging composers whose talent has outpaced what they can otherwise afford to pursue.

## One scholarship, several ways to fund it

The site has one real job: make the case for the scholarship in Rahim's own words, then make it simple to actually support it. Giving is split by intent, general donations, the scholarship fund specifically, and a membership at $5 a month or $50 a year that comes with a signed CD, so a donor can support the mission at whatever level fits, from a recurring membership up to giving through a donor-advised fund.

## A committee, not just a name

The scholarship's credibility rests on more than Rahim's own reputation. The site lists its review committee in full, ethnomusicologists, music theorists, conductors, and working musicians, because a scholarship that names its judges is one that can be trusted to actually judge.

The organisation is new and still building its history of programs and events, so the site is built to fill in as that happens rather than promising a backlog that does not exist yet.

Built on Astro, for a small, young nonprofit that needed something fast and fully custom rather than a template it would grow out of.
