The Dermatology & Skin Cancer Center of New Mexico has specialized in medical dermatology since 2007. Many patients arrive at its website because they are worried about something they have found on their skin.

Someone in that position is not casually browsing. They need to know whether the practice treats their concern, whether it accepts their insurance, and how to arrange an appointment. The writing must be informative without making an anxious reader more alarmed.

We organized the site around a patient center that answers those questions directly and keeps administrative information easy to find. Clinical material uses plain language while retaining the detail patients need before contacting the office.

The current commission will be the third time the practice has trusted MIS to redesign its website. That continuing relationship is the most meaningful evidence from the project, and the new redesign will be documented after it launches.
