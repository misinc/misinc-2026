Advanced Skylight Services has worked with MIS for more than twenty years. The company is New Mexico’s premier skylight manufacturer and the exclusive maker of Skylight Star® custom skylights.

In 2025, ownership passed to the founder’s daughter, Rheanna, and we rebuilt the site.

A generational handover is a natural point for a business to reconsider how it presents itself. The old site had not failed, but the person now leading the company had a different view of where it was going. Two decades of history with the business meant we could begin with that context instead of relearning it.

## Built for the way skylights are chosen

Architects specify skylights, contractors install them, and homeowners live under them. An architect wants dimensions, shapes, and specifications. A contractor needs confidence that the product will fit and arrive. A homeowner wants to imagine it in a room.

The new structure gives each reader useful information without forcing everyone through the same sales path.

## A real product catalog

The product range includes barrel vaults, clustered skylights, curb mount, insulated curb, operable, pyramidal, ridge vaults, self-flashing, and special shapes. This is a catalog, not a short services list. Specifications and options appear where a professional expects them, while galleries make the range understandable to everyone else.

[Webflow](/platforms/webflow) lets the team add products and project photography as the next generation of the business develops its own body of work.
